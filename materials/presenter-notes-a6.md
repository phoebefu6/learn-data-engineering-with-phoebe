# Presenter notes - Leader session 6: The DE org and roadmap

**Audience:** CxO, VPs, managers. No code. 45 min, thinking mode. Final leader session.
**One-line goal:** they leave able to staff a data team correctly and sequence the build crawl-walk-run.

## Run of show (45 min)
- **0-3 Welcome.** Frame as the closing session: "You know what DE does - now, who does it and in what order?" Note this is the honest-close session with a real gap list.
- **3-20 Who does what (Part 1).** Walk the roles diagram - DE in the center feeding everyone. Spend extra time on the analytics engineer (the role leaders miss). Tell the "senior DS doing junior plumbing" story. Open card 1 live.
- **20-42 The crawl-walk-run roadmap (Part 2).** Walk the strip. Hammer: earn each stage. Tell the "streaming before reliable batch" story. Then Part 3 handoff table + the honest "what we did NOT cover" list.
- **42-45 Q&A + close.** Congratulate them on finishing the leader track. Point builders to b1-b10.

## Preflight
- Open a6 in projector zoom; check the roles diagram arrows read clearly.
- Know your own org's current stage (crawl/walk/run) so you can model the honest self-assessment.
- Have the sibling-course URLs handy (warehouse, dataops, sql) for the handoff table.
- Be ready to name which of the five roles your audience's org is missing.

## Never cut
- The analytics-engineer role + why it is often the highest-leverage hire.
- Crawl-walk-run and the "don't skip stages" discipline (core of the session).
- The honest "what this course did NOT cover" list - it is the credibility close.

## Cut if long
- The platform/infra role detail (mention in the diagram, don't dwell).
- The quarterly-questions self-study card (point to homework instead).
- Trim the handoff table to two rows (warehouse + dataops) if time is tight.

## Likely exec questions
- **"How many data engineers do we need?"** Depends on stage. At crawl, often one or two plus an analytics engineer beats five specialists. Sequence hiring to the roadmap.
- **"Everyone wants real-time. Why not build it?"** Only if batch is already trustworthy and real-time earns its 5-10x cost (session a3). Streaming on a shaky base is faster wrong answers.
- **"What's an analytics engineer vs a data engineer again?"** DE builds the pipes; analytics engineer models the landed data (dbt) into trusted tables. The bridge to the warehouse.
- **"What do we learn next?"** Warehouse (modeling), dataops (running it), sql (querying). Builders take the b1-b10 hands-on track of this same course.
- **"Is this course enough to run our data effort?"** For leading it, yes - you now have the vocabulary. Building it is the builder track and the sibling courses.
